Questions that work well:
- What did you last ask a family member to fix on your phone?
- What happened? How long did you wait for help?
- Is your phone in Spanish or English? Why?
- Would you let an app see your screen if it only reads it on the phone? What would worry you?
- Do you prefer to hear instructions, read them, or both?

## Small test (3 to 5 people)

1. Settings → turn on "Save anonymous usage counts on this phone".
2. Set WhatsApp photo access to **None/Ninguno** before each person.
3. Without Lola: ask them to let WhatsApp see their photos. Time it with a stopwatch; stop at 5 minutes. Write down whether they finished.
4. With Lola: same task, fresh setup. The app logs time, hints, "I'm lost", wrong turns.
5. Export the usage log (Settings → Export usage log), then `cd backend && npm run impact -- usage-log.jsonl`.
