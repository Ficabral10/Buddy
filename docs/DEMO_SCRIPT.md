# Demo script (2 minutes, demo first)

**Setup:** iPhone in Spanish, Do Not Disturb on, WhatsApp photo access set to **Ninguno**, Lola
opened from the Home Screen, family contact "Ana" saved, screen mirrored. Have two screenshots ready
in Photos: the **Privacidad y seguridad** screen and a fake scam text (see below). Backend and tunnel
running; run the whole flow once before judging so the voice is cached.

| Time | Presenter says | On the phone |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "Rosa is 72. She speaks Spanish. Every time her iPhone needs a setting changed, she waits for her grandkids. Today WhatsApp can't see her photos." | Lola's home screen. |
| 0:12 | "She just asks." | Tap **Toca y habla**: "WhatsApp no ve mis fotos." Lola (voice): "Quieres que WhatsApp pueda ver tus fotos. ¿Es así?" Tap **Sí**. |
| 0:25 | "Lola tells her one step at a time, naming the exact words on her phone. She does every tap herself." | Lola: "Ve a la pantalla de inicio y abre Ajustes." Go to Settings; come back; Lola: "¿Ya lo hiciste?" → **Sí**. |
| 0:40 | "iPhones don't let any app point at buttons in Settings. So when she's unsure, she sends a screenshot, and Lola reads it right on the phone and circles the exact button." | **Enviar una captura** → pick the Privacidad screenshot → red ring on **Fotos**, "👆 Toca aquí". |
| 1:00 | "If she gets lost, she says so, and Claude explains it differently, but only with steps from our checked library." | Say "estoy perdida" → new explanation naming "Fotos". Finish the steps → "Eso es todo." → **Escribirle a Ana por WhatsApp** → she taps Send. |
| 1:20 | "Older adults are the top target for phone scams. Rosa got this text." | Home → **¿Es una estafa?** → pick the scam screenshot → **Revisar** → red verdict, spoken: "Esto parece una estafa… No toques el enlace…" → **Preguntarle a Ana**. |
| 1:40 | "Claude understands her and explains; code checks every answer: real steps only, exact labels, never 'tap the link' or 'give a code'. Cartesia is her voice in Spanish. Screens are read on the phone." | (Optional) show the README guardrail table. |
| 1:55 | "Lola: she does it herself, in her language, and stays safe." | |

**Scam text for the demo** (type it into Notes and screenshot it, or paste into the box):
> USPS: Tu paquete está retenido. Paga $1.99 hoy mismo en https://usps-entrega.co/pago o será devuelto.

## Backup plan
- Network fails → library replies and the phone's built-in voice keep the flow going.
- Screenshot reading slow → use **Siguiente** to move on and say "she can also send a screenshot".
- Everything fails → play the recorded video (record it on the demo phone after rehearsal).

## Rehearse
Three full runs, timed. Reset WhatsApp photo access to **Ninguno** between runs.
